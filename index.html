Operator guide

Review your first packet

  1. Compare the two sources. Open the revision case. Read the revision in the drawing and in the enquiry.
  2. Check how the evidence was obtained. View the OCR transcript or rerun recognition. Switch on detected-hole markers to inspect the geometry.
  3. Investigate the hold. Review the exception, look up the relevant reference and refer it with a short reason.
  4. Record the customer's clarification. Select the supplied response, explain the decision, and compare the original and effective values.
  5. Pass it to estimating. Download the handoff, open the estimating inbox and import it. Try the incomplete packet to see how a hold affects intake.
