# How the demonstration maps to the KTP role

The NTU role profile describes seven project stages. This guide gives a hiring reviewer an observable question for each stage and identifies the evidence the industrial project would still need. The public quotation workflow uses fictional enquiry material. A separate image reader accepts a visitor’s file or camera frame for provisional transcription. The proposed company work would require access to staff, authorised customer documents and business systems. [NTU role profile, page 1](https://vacancies.ntu.ac.uk/Job/GetJobAdvertDocument?Id=8412&JobId=2643) · [KTP project brief](https://iuk-ktp.org.uk/job/ai-automation-solutions-developer/)

**Try it as an estimator.** Choose a sample, compare the drawing with the enquiry and inspect the OCR reading. Follow a flagged discrepancy to its source, search the supplied clauses and record a customer clarification if the case includes one. Then export the review draft or the internal estimating handoff allowed by the case state. The commercial costing and formal quote release steps sit downstream of this prototype. ## Questions for a hiring review

1. Can you locate the original evidence for a disputed requirement without relying on the system’s interpretation?
2. Can you tell which observations came from recognising pixels, reading text, retrieving records or applying an explicit rule?
3. When evidence is missing or contradictory, does the handoff make clear what the estimator needs to obtain?
4. Which test would you add before using an unfamiliar customer’s drawing or connecting the prototype to a controlled document library?
5. Why does a model-assisted reading of a visitor’s camera image require human review even when both readers agree on every value?

The role also asks for a record of collaboration, communication, independent delivery and applying research to real problems. A public repository gives the panel software, design decisions and test evidence to discuss. Workplace examples, references and an observed handover would supply the evidence for those interpersonal and operational criteria.
