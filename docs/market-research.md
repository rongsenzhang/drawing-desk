# Midland Aerospace and the quotation market

**Research date: 20 November 2026.** Drawing Desk explores a decision an estimator faces before pricing a job: are the customer’s drawing, enquiry and supporting specifications consistent enough to proceed, or is a clarification needed? NTU describes this need in a 32-month project with Midland Aerospace. The company’s public pages provide context for the question. The examples in Drawing Desk are independently authored. [NTU vacancy](https://vacancies.ntu.ac.uk/Job/JobDetail?JobId=2643) · [KTP project description](https://iuk-ktp.org.uk/job/ai-automation-solutions-developer/)

## The manufacturing business

Midland Aerospace manufactures precision components and assemblies in Sutton-in-Ashfield. Its core commercial aerospace markets include aftermarket spares and Airbus passenger-to-freighter conversions. The company reports that exports account for over 70% of sales. It joined the Calder Group in 2008 and received a King’s Award for Enterprise in International Trade in 2025. [Company overview](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/) · [Company award announcement](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/documents/KA_Press_Release.pdf)

Customers can bring their own designs for manufacture. Midland offers machining, fabrication, heat treatment, chemical processing, painting, non-destructive testing and assembly. Its inspection team checks both work in progress and completed parts. It also supports urgent aircraft-on-ground requests from estimating through manufacture. An estimator may therefore need to identify a finishing or inspection requirement in the customer’s written notes as well as recognise features on a drawing. That last point is a design inference from the published services. [Capabilities and services](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/capabilities-and-services/) · [About Midland](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/about-us/)

### What the public material establishes about quotations

| Public evidence | Consequence for this demonstration |
| --- | --- |
| Buyers provide drawings, specifications and instructions. The supply terms distinguish a seller’s quotation from formal acceptance of an order. | Let the reviewer trace a requirement across a drawing and an enquiry. Label the result as preparation for estimating. [Supply terms, §§3.2 and 5.2](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/documents/conditions-of-supply.pdf) |
| Midland describes a three-stream contract review for purchase orders. It does not name the streams or publish the sequence for reviewing incoming enquiries. | Keep any simulated review steps explicit. Their names and sequence are product design choices. [Project management services](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/capabilities-and-services/) |
| The purchase terms discuss supplier quality documentation and traceability. In those terms Midland is the **buyer**. | Treat these clauses as evidence of the wider information environment. Only the fictional customer’s stated requirements apply to the sample enquiry. [Purchase terms, §§1 and 5](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/documents/conditions-of-purchase.pdf) |
| Midland publishes sales, engineering, production and quality contacts. | Different people may need the same evidence to answer different questions. The public directory establishes functions, while authority to approve a real quotation would require discovery with staff. [Contact directory](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/contact/) |

### A real example of why document identity matters

At the review date, two routes on the company website led to heat-treatment certificates with different numbers and expiry dates. The Approvals page linked a certificate expiring **31 August 2026**. The Documents page linked one expiring **29 February 2028**. Both certificates refer readers to the qualified manufacturers list for the applicable service scope. A reviewer seeing only the earlier PDF could draw the wrong conclusion about current cover. A useful review tool keeps the source, date and scope visible and refers competing records to a qualified reviewer. [Certificate linked from Approvals](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/documents/Certificate_Nadcap_Heat_Treating.pdf) · [Certificate linked from Documents](https://www.midlandaerospace.com/documents/certificate-HT-LETTER.pdf)

## Software alternatives

These are comparisons of published product scope. They provide useful benchmarks for a build-or-buy investigation. No supplier trial, procurement assessment or performance comparison was conducted.

| Product | What the vendor documents | Design choice for Drawing Desk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paperless Parts | Analyses CAD geometry to drive routing and costing, supports shop-specific pricing templates, and flags possible manufacturing issues. | Keep commercial assumptions separate from the evidence used to interpret a requirement. An industrial cost estimate needs local rates and process knowledge. [Pricing and costing](https://www.paperlessparts.com/pricing-costing-automation/) |
| Werk24 | Documents a structured representation of dimensions across multiple drawing conventions and exposes related features for tolerances, title blocks and revision tables. | Link an extracted value to its source and restrict claims to the conventions actually tested. Specialist extraction is a candidate component in a larger workflow. [Measures documentation](https://docs.werk24.io/features/measures.html) |
| DigiFabster | Accepts CAD models for automated feature recognition and configurable pricing. Its documented manual-review mode covers non-CAD submissions such as PDFs and images. | Give reviewers a meaningful outcome when the evidence cannot support a decision. A documented clarification can move a job forward. [Instant quoting and manual review](https://digifabster.com/products/instant-quoting-solution/) |

For the vacancy, the useful question is how these capabilities fit together when an actual enquiry contains several documents. Drawing Desk focuses on the point where a person can check the evidence, identify conflicts and decide what an estimator still needs. Measuring the time saved or the number of omissions caught would require comparable jobs and independent human review. The review checked linked documents for relevance and examined the pertinent commercial clauses and certificate dates. It did not include a formal certification audit, video footage, interviews, private systems or drafts outside the public navigation. The site supplied no XML sitemap at its standard address.
